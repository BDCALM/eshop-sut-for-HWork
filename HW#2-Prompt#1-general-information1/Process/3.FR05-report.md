# FR-05 Process Report — Product Listing and Search

## Scope

FR-05 covers displaying the product list, searching by product name, and
observing loading, error, and empty-result states. Product detail, cart,
checkout, and product administration are out of scope.

## Step A — Feature analysis and equivalence partitioning

Gate A is **Approved** on 2026-10-18. The approved analysis identifies:

- Search value: empty or non-empty text.
- Product/request state: loading, successful response with products,
  successful response with no products, and request error.
- Output: product list/cards, empty result rendering, or error rendering.
- The maximum search length and accepted-character constraints remain
  unresolved; no unsupported upper boundary is invented.

The approved classes and domain test cases are recorded in
[Domain-testing-report.md](../Submission/reports/main%20reports%20%28need%20md%20%2B%20pdf%29/Domain-testing-report.md).

## Step B — Boundary Value Analysis

Two evidence-backed ordered domains were selected:

1. Search value length: lower boundary `0` characters, then `1` character,
   with `alpha = 1`. The upper boundary is unresolved because no maximum
   length is specified by the available evidence.
2. Product result cardinality: `0`, `1`, and `2+` results. The representative
   boundary probes are `0`, `1`, and `2` results.

Seven BVA probes were designed:

| BVA-TC-FR05-02 | Search length | One-character matching fragment | Matching product result is displayed. | Passed; `FR05-03-searchOneChar.png` |
| BVA-TC-FR05-03 | Search length | Two-character matching fragment | Matching product result is displayed; this is the `LB + 1` probe. | Passed; `FR05-02-searchEmpty.png` |
